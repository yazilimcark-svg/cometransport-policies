ComeTransport

Account & Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

ComeTransport respects your privacy and provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

This policy explains how you can request the deletion of your ComeTransport account and what happens after a deletion request.

2. How to Delete Your Account

If account deletion is available directly within the ComeTransport application, you can start the process from your account or profile settings.

General process:
  1. Open the ComeTransport application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your account or profile settings.
  4. Select the account deletion option.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps displayed by the application.

If the in-app deletion feature is temporarily unavailable, you may contact ComeTransport through the official support contact method provided by the application or website and request account deletion.

3. What Happens When You Request Deletion

After a valid deletion request is received and processed, ComeTransport will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law.

Depending on the nature of the data, this may include:

  • Account and profile information
  • Personal contact information
  • User preferences
  • Transportation requests associated with the account
  • Offers or transportation-related activity associated with the account
  • Messages and other user-generated information where deletion is technically and legally possible

4. Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required or permitted by applicable law.

This may include information necessary for:

  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Resolving disputes
  • Enforcing agreements
  • Maintaining legally required records

Where retention is legally required, the retained information will be limited to the information necessary for the applicable purpose and protected appropriately.

5. Effect on User Content

Deleting an account may remove or anonymize content associated with that account where technically and legally appropriate.

Certain content may remain in anonymized or aggregated form where it can no longer reasonably be associated with the deleted account.

6. Active Transactions or Arrangements

If you have an ongoing transportation arrangement with another user, deleting your account may affect communication, requests, offers, or other platform functionality associated with that arrangement.

Users should take any necessary steps to complete or document their arrangements before requesting account deletion.

7. Subscriptions

Deleting a ComeTransport account does not necessarily cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

If you have an active subscription purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play, you should manage or cancel that subscription through the applicable app store account.

Important: Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate processes.

8. Processing Time

ComeTransport will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, taking into account technical requirements, applicable law, security procedures, and the scope of the request.

9. Verification

ComeTransport may take reasonable steps to verify that a deletion request was submitted by the account owner or an authorized person.

This verification is intended to prevent unauthorized deletion of another person's account.

10. Re-registration

After an account has been deleted, you may create a new ComeTransport account in the future, subject to the rules and eligibility requirements applicable at that time.

11. Contact

If you cannot access your account or cannot use the account deletion function, please contact ComeTransport through the official support contact method provided within the application or on the official ComeTransport website.

12. Changes to This Policy

ComeTransport may update this Account & Data Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service, legal requirements, or account-management functionality.
