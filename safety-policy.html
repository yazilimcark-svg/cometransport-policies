Safety Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

ComeTransport is a transportation and service marketplace that connects people and businesses that need transportation with people and businesses that can provide transportation services.

Safety is an important part of the ComeTransport community. This Safety Policy explains the safety expectations that apply when using the platform, communicating with other users, creating transportation requests, offering transportation services, and arranging the handover of goods.

ComeTransport provides a digital platform for connecting users. Unless expressly stated otherwise, ComeTransport does not itself provide, perform, or control transportation services.

1. General Safety Responsibilities

Every ComeTransport user is responsible for using reasonable care and judgment.

  • Provide accurate information about yourself and your transportation activity.
  • Communicate honestly and respectfully.
  • Follow applicable laws and regulations.
  • Do not knowingly create unsafe transportation arrangements.
  • Do not use ComeTransport for illegal or prohibited activities.
  • Report serious safety concerns when appropriate.

2. Safety for Transportation Requesters

Users requesting transportation should provide accurate and sufficient information about their request.

  • Provide accurate pickup and destination information.
  • Describe the goods or items accurately.
  • Disclose relevant characteristics that may affect safe transportation.
  • Do not conceal dangerous, illegal, restricted, or prohibited items.
  • Use appropriate packaging where applicable.
  • Do not ask another user to transport an item in an unsafe or unlawful manner.
Never intentionally hide information about an item when that information could affect the safety or legality of its transportation.

3. Safety for Transportation Providers

Transportation providers are responsible for determining whether they can safely and legally perform a transportation request.

  • Review a request before accepting it.
  • Only accept transportation that you can safely perform.
  • Use an appropriate and legally permitted vehicle or transportation method.
  • Follow applicable traffic and transportation laws.
  • Maintain any licenses, permits, insurance, or qualifications required by applicable law.
  • Do not knowingly transport prohibited or illegal items.
  • Do not operate a vehicle while impaired or excessively fatigued.
  • Secure transported goods appropriately where applicable.

4. Vehicle and Transportation Safety

Where transportation involves a vehicle, the transportation provider is responsible for ensuring that the vehicle is appropriate, properly maintained, and legally permitted for the intended use.

ComeTransport does not independently guarantee the condition, safety, maintenance, licensing, insurance, or suitability of a vehicle used by another user.

5. Meeting and Handover Safety

Users may need to meet each other to coordinate transportation or hand over goods. Users should take reasonable precautions during these interactions.

  • When practical, use public or appropriate meeting locations.
  • Prefer reasonably well-lit locations when possible.
  • Do not enter a private location if you feel unsafe.
  • Do not invite an unknown person into your home solely because of a transportation arrangement.
  • Keep unnecessary personal information private.
  • Consider informing a trusted person about an in-person meeting when appropriate.

6. Location and Privacy Safety

Depending on the application's features and permissions granted by the user, ComeTransport may use location-related information to support transportation matching, pickup and destination coordination, or other platform functionality.

Users should avoid unnecessarily sharing sensitive personal information, including passwords, financial credentials, identity documents, or other information that is not required for the intended service.

Users must respect the privacy of other users and must not misuse, publish, or distribute another person's personal information without an appropriate lawful basis.

7. Communication Safety

ComeTransport may provide communication features that allow users to discuss transportation requests and services.

Users should exercise appropriate caution when communicating with people they do not personally know.

Users must not use ComeTransport communications to threaten, intimidate, harass, exploit, defraud, or otherwise harm another person.

8. Prohibited and Dangerous Activities

ComeTransport must not be used to facilitate illegal, dangerous, or prohibited activities.

  • Transporting illegal goods.
  • Transporting prohibited weapons or explosives.
  • Transporting hazardous materials in violation of applicable requirements.
  • Human trafficking or exploitation.
  • Fraudulent transportation arrangements.
  • Threats, violence, or intimidation.
  • Attempting to evade applicable legal requirements.
  • Misrepresenting the nature or contents of transported items.

Additional restrictions are described in the ComeTransport Acceptable Use Policy and Prohibited Items Policy.

9. Children and Minors

Users must not use ComeTransport to exploit, endanger, solicit, or otherwise place minors in inappropriate or unsafe situations.

Any transportation involving minors must comply with applicable laws and appropriate safety requirements.

10. Emergencies

ComeTransport is not an emergency response service.

If you are experiencing immediate danger, an accident, a medical emergency, a threat of violence, or another urgent situation, contact the appropriate local emergency services or authorities immediately.

Users should not wait for ComeTransport support before contacting emergency services when immediate assistance is required.

11. Reporting Safety Concerns

Users should report serious safety concerns, threats, harassment, fraud, prohibited activity, or other violations through the reporting or support mechanisms made available by ComeTransport.

Reports should contain accurate information and, where appropriate, relevant details that can help ComeTransport understand the situation.

ComeTransport may review reports and take appropriate action under its policies and applicable law.

12. Blocking and Avoiding Users

Where blocking or similar functionality is available, users may use it to limit unwanted interactions with another user.

If an interaction makes you feel unsafe, you should end the interaction when reasonably possible and report the situation to ComeTransport or the appropriate authorities.

13. Identity and Verification

ComeTransport may offer verification features depending on the version of the platform and the services available in a particular market.

A verification indicator, profile, rating, review, or other platform signal does not guarantee that a user is safe, trustworthy, licensed, insured, qualified, or suitable for a particular transportation arrangement.

14. Ratings and Reviews

Where ratings or reviews are available, users should provide honest, relevant, and experience-based feedback.

Ratings and reviews must not be used to threaten, extort, discriminate against, harass, or intentionally mislead another user.

15. ComeTransport's Role and Limitations

ComeTransport provides technology that helps users discover, communicate with, and connect with other users for transportation-related opportunities.

Transportation services may be provided by independent users or businesses. ComeTransport therefore cannot guarantee that every user will behave safely, honestly, legally, or professionally.

Unless a specific verification service is expressly provided and described by ComeTransport, ComeTransport does not guarantee a user's identity, background, qualifications, licensing, insurance, vehicle condition, financial standing, or conduct.

16. Cooperation With Authorities

ComeTransport may cooperate with law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other competent authorities when required or permitted by applicable law.

Information may be disclosed in accordance with the ComeTransport Privacy Policy and applicable legal requirements.

17. No Transportation Payment System

ComeTransport does not provide a payment system for transportation services.

ComeTransport does not collect, hold, transfer, process, or settle transportation fees between users.

ComeTransport does not take commissions from transportation fees and does not provide escrow or payment settlement for transportation arrangements.

The only paid service offered by ComeTransport is an optional Premium subscription for digital features within the application.

Premium subscriptions are processed through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing system.

18. Safety Enforcement

ComeTransport may take appropriate action when it reasonably believes that a user has violated this Safety Policy or another platform policy.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, actions may include:

  • Removing content.
  • Restricting platform features.
  • Removing or limiting transportation listings.
  • Suspending an account.
  • Terminating an account.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally permitted or required.
  • Cooperating with appropriate authorities.

19. No Guarantee of Safety

The measures described in this policy are intended to support safer use of ComeTransport. They cannot eliminate every risk associated with transportation services or interactions between users.

Users remain responsible for exercising appropriate judgment and taking reasonable precautions based on their individual circumstances.

20. Relationship With Other Policies

This Safety Policy should be read together with the ComeTransport Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, Community Guidelines, and Prohibited Items Policy.

21. Changes to This Safety Policy

ComeTransport may update this Safety Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, applicable law, safety practices, or available features.

The updated version will be published through the applicable ComeTransport policy page with an updated revision date where appropriate.

22. Contact

For safety concerns, policy questions, or reports relating to ComeTransport, users should use the official support or contact method provided by ComeTransport.
