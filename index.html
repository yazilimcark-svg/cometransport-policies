About ComeTransport

ComeTransport is a platform that connects people and businesses seeking transportation services with people and businesses able to provide transportation services.

ComeTransport does not operate transportation services itself and does not collect, hold, transfer, or settle transportation fees between users.

The only paid service provided through the platform is the optional Premium subscription, which is processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play.
