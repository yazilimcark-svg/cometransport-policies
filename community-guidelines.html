ComeTransport

Community Guidelines

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Our Community

ComeTransport is a platform designed to connect people and businesses that need transportation with people and businesses that may provide transportation services.

A safe, respectful, and trustworthy community is important for the platform to function effectively.

These Community Guidelines apply to profiles, transportation requests, offers, messages, reviews, comments, and other user interactions on ComeTransport.

2. Be Respectful

Treat other users, businesses, and service providers respectfully.

Do not use ComeTransport to harass, threaten, intimidate, humiliate, or deliberately target another person.

3. Be Honest

Information shared through ComeTransport should be accurate and not intentionally misleading.

This includes:

  • Your identity or business identity.
  • Your location.
  • Transportation requirements.
  • Vehicle or service information.
  • Availability.
  • Prices or other agreed conditions.
  • Information about goods being transported.

4. Authentic Profiles

Users should represent themselves or their businesses accurately.

Do not impersonate another person, company, organization, or transportation provider.

Do not create misleading profiles intended to deceive other users.

5. Safe Communication

Use ComeTransport's communication features primarily for legitimate discussions related to transportation needs and services.

Do not send messages that contain threats, scams, malicious links, harassment, or intentionally harmful content.

Safety first: Do not share passwords, verification codes, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information with another user.

6. No Harassment or Threats

The following behavior is not permitted:

  • Threatening another user.
  • Repeated unwanted contact.
  • Stalking or monitoring another person.
  • Intimidation.
  • Sexual harassment.
  • Abusive or degrading communications.
  • Encouraging others to target or harm a user.

7. Hate and Discrimination

ComeTransport should not be used to promote hatred, violence, or unlawful discrimination against individuals or groups.

Content that encourages violence or serious harm may be removed and may result in account restrictions.

8. No Fraud or Scams

Users must not use the community to deceive other users or obtain money, goods, services, or personal information through fraudulent methods.

Examples include:

  • Fake transportation requests.
  • Fake service offers.
  • False identities.
  • Phishing attempts.
  • Fake payment requests.
  • Fraudulent documents.
  • Promises made with no intention of performing them.

9. No Spam

Do not use ComeTransport to send large volumes of unwanted messages or repetitive promotional content.

Automated messaging, bulk advertising, and other abusive promotional activity may be restricted.

10. Respect Privacy

Do not publish or distribute another person's private information without appropriate authorization.

This includes personal addresses, phone numbers, identification documents, financial information, private conversations, or other sensitive information.

11. User Content

Users are responsible for the content they publish or send through ComeTransport.

Content must comply with applicable laws and the ComeTransport Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and Prohibited Items Policy.

12. Transportation Safety

Users should communicate honestly about information that may materially affect the safe or lawful completion of a transportation arrangement.

Do not intentionally hide dangerous, prohibited, restricted, or otherwise relevant information about goods or transportation requirements.

13. Reviews and Ratings

Where ratings or reviews are available, users should provide honest feedback based on genuine interactions or completed arrangements.

Users must not manipulate ratings through fake accounts, coordinated activity, threats, bribery, or other deceptive methods.

14. Commercial Promotion

Businesses and transportation providers may use appropriate platform features to present legitimate services.

Promotional activity must not become spam, fraud, misleading advertising, or an attempt to circumvent platform restrictions.

15. Prohibited Content

Users must not upload or distribute content that is unlawful, fraudulent, threatening, abusive, or otherwise prohibited under ComeTransport policies.

Content involving illegal goods, exploitation, serious threats, malicious software, or other unlawful activity may be removed.

16. Reporting Problems

If you encounter harassment, fraud, suspicious activity, prohibited content, or another violation of these Guidelines, use the available reporting tools within ComeTransport or contact the official ComeTransport support channel.

When reporting an issue, provide enough information to help ComeTransport understand and investigate the situation.

17. Enforcement

ComeTransport may review reports and take reasonable action when these Guidelines or other platform policies are violated.

Possible actions may include:

  • Removing content.
  • Restricting communication features.
  • Limiting account functionality.
  • Suspending an account.
  • Terminating an account.
  • Blocking abusive activity.
  • Reporting suspected unlawful activity where appropriate.

18. Safety Limitations

ComeTransport provides tools intended to help users connect, but no platform feature can guarantee the identity, honesty, safety, licensing, insurance, or behavior of another user.

Users should exercise appropriate judgment and take reasonable precautions when interacting with people or businesses they do not know.

19. Relationship With Other Policies

These Community Guidelines are part of the broader ComeTransport rules and should be read together with the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Safety Policy, and Prohibited Items Policy.

20. Changes to These Guidelines

ComeTransport may update these Guidelines when necessary to reflect changes in the Service, community standards, safety practices, or applicable requirements.

21. Contact

If you have questions about these Community Guidelines or need to report a violation, please contact ComeTransport through the official support contact method provided within the application or on the official ComeTransport website.
