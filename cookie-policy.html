2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. They can help websites remember information, maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand how services are used.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, SDKs, and other technologies that perform functions similar to cookies.
