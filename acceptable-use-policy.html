ComeTransport

Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy explains the rules that apply when using ComeTransport.

ComeTransport is intended to provide a safe and useful environment where people and businesses can connect regarding transportation needs and transportation services.

This policy applies to all users, accounts, listings, requests, offers, messages, profiles, and other activity on the Service.

2. General Requirements

When using ComeTransport, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and reasonably current information.
  • Use the Service only for lawful purposes.
  • Respect other users and businesses.
  • Comply with applicable transportation and safety requirements.
  • Respect applicable licensing, insurance, tax, and regulatory obligations.
  • Protect your account credentials.
  • Use the platform honestly and in good faith.

3. Prohibited Illegal Activities

You must not use ComeTransport to facilitate, promote, organize, or participate in unlawful activity.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Fraud or financial scams.
  • Money laundering or attempts to conceal unlawful funds.
  • Identity theft or impersonation.
  • Trafficking or exploitation of people.
  • Distribution of illegal goods or substances.
  • Activities prohibited by applicable sanctions or export-control laws.
  • Unauthorized transportation activities where a license or permit is legally required.

4. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Users must not intentionally provide false, deceptive, or misleading information.

This includes:

  • Fake names or identities.
  • False business information.
  • Fake transportation requests.
  • Fake offers or availability.
  • Misleading vehicle or service information.
  • False location information.
  • Attempts to manipulate ratings or reviews.

5. Harassment and Abuse

ComeTransport must not be used to threaten, harass, intimidate, stalk, abuse, or deliberately harm another person.

Users must not send unwanted communications that are abusive, threatening, excessively disruptive, or otherwise inappropriate.

6. Hate, Discrimination, and Violence

Users must not use ComeTransport to promote violence against people or groups or to encourage unlawful discrimination or harassment.

Content that threatens serious physical harm or encourages violent criminal activity may be removed and may result in account restrictions.

7. Dangerous or Prohibited Goods

ComeTransport must not be used to arrange transportation of goods that are illegal, prohibited, or otherwise unsafe to transport.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws and regulations concerning dangerous goods, restricted goods, hazardous materials, customs requirements, and transportation restrictions.

Important: Users should never assume that an item is permitted merely because the platform allows a transportation request to be created.

8. Personal Information

Users must respect the privacy of other users.

You must not collect, publish, sell, distribute, or misuse another user's personal information without appropriate authorization or a lawful basis.

This includes phone numbers, addresses, identification documents, financial information, private communications, and other sensitive information.

9. Spam and Unwanted Promotion

Users must not use ComeTransport to send spam or conduct abusive promotional campaigns.

Examples include:

  • Mass unsolicited messages.
  • Repeated unwanted advertisements.
  • Automated promotional activity.
  • Scam links.
  • Manipulative referral activity.
  • Attempts to redirect users through deceptive methods.

10. Platform Security

You must not attempt to compromise, damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to ComeTransport or its related systems.

This includes:

  • Attempting to access another user's account.
  • Bypassing security controls.
  • Introducing malicious software.
  • Scraping data through unauthorized automated methods.
  • Overloading or interfering with the Service.
  • Testing vulnerabilities without authorization.

11. Automated Access

Automated tools, bots, scripts, crawlers, or similar technologies must not be used to access or interact with ComeTransport in a way that violates the Service's technical restrictions or harms the platform.

12. Transportation Provider Responsibilities

Users offering transportation services are responsible for ensuring that they are legally authorized to provide those services.

Where applicable, providers should maintain appropriate licenses, permits, insurance, vehicle documentation, and other legally required qualifications.

ComeTransport does not independently guarantee that every provider satisfies these requirements.

13. Transportation Requester Responsibilities

Users requesting transportation should provide accurate information about the transportation requirement.

Users should not knowingly conceal information that could materially affect safety, legality, pricing, or the ability to perform the transportation service.

14. Circumvention

Users must not deliberately abuse ComeTransport features to evade safety systems, account restrictions, security measures, or other platform rules.

Creating multiple accounts for the purpose of avoiding a suspension or restriction may result in additional account action.

15. Enforcement

ComeTransport may review reported violations and take reasonable action where appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting content.
  • Limiting account functionality.
  • Suspending an account.
  • Terminating an account.
  • Blocking abusive technical activity.
  • Reporting suspected unlawful activity where legally appropriate.

16. Reporting Violations

If you believe that another user or piece of content violates this policy, you may report it through available reporting tools within ComeTransport or through the official support contact method.

Reports should contain enough information to allow ComeTransport to understand and review the reported issue.

17. No Guarantee of User Conduct

ComeTransport cannot guarantee that every user will comply with this policy.

Users should exercise appropriate caution when interacting with people or businesses they do not know.

18. Relationship With Other Policies

This Acceptable Use Policy forms part of the ComeTransport Terms of Service.

Other policies, including the Privacy Policy, Safety Policy, and Prohibited Items Policy, may contain additional requirements.

19. Changes to This Policy

ComeTransport may update this policy when necessary to reflect changes in the Service, applicable laws, safety requirements, or platform practices.

20. Contact

If you have questions about this Acceptable Use Policy or wish to report a potential violation, please contact ComeTransport through the official support contact method provided within the application or on the official ComeTransport website.
