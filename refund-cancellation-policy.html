Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

ComeTransport offers Premium subscriptions as its only paid service. ComeTransport does not charge users transportation fees and does not process payments for transportation services between users.

1. Scope

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellation and refund matters relating to ComeTransport Premium subscriptions are handled.

This policy applies only to paid Premium subscription purchases made through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing system.

2. No Transportation Payment Refunds

ComeTransport does not operate a payment system for transportation services.

ComeTransport does not collect, process, hold, or settle transportation fees between users.

Therefore, ComeTransport does not provide refunds for transportation fees because transportation fees are not paid to or processed by ComeTransport.

3. Premium Subscription

Premium is an optional paid subscription that may provide access to additional digital features within the ComeTransport application.

The subscription price, billing period, trial terms, and renewal conditions are presented through the applicable app marketplace before purchase.

4. How Subscription Cancellation Works

Premium subscriptions purchased through the ComeTransport mobile application are managed through the applicable app marketplace.

  • Apple: Subscription management and cancellation are handled through the Apple App Store subscription settings.
  • Google: Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Google Play subscription settings.

Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewal. Depending on the applicable marketplace rules, cancellation may not immediately end access to Premium features that have already been purchased for the current billing period.

5. Refund Requests

Because Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play, refund requests are generally subject to the refund rules and procedures of the marketplace through which the purchase was made.

ComeTransport does not control the payment processing or refund decisions of Apple or Google.

A request to cancel a subscription and a request for a refund are not necessarily the same thing. Cancelling future renewal does not automatically mean that a payment already made will be refunded.

6. Apple Purchases

If a Premium subscription was purchased through Apple's App Store, users should use Apple's applicable subscription and refund procedures for cancellation or refund requests.

Any refund decision for an Apple-processed purchase is subject to Apple's applicable policies and procedures.

7. Google Play Purchases

If a Premium subscription was purchased through Google Play, users should use Google's applicable subscription and refund procedures for cancellation or refund requests.

Any refund decision for a Google Play-processed purchase is subject to Google's applicable policies and procedures.

8. Free Trials

ComeTransport may offer a free trial for Premium where supported.

If a free trial automatically converts into a paid subscription, the applicable renewal conditions will be shown before or during the subscription process in accordance with marketplace requirements.

Users who do not want a trial to convert into a paid subscription should cancel according to the applicable Apple or Google subscription management procedure before the trial period ends.

9. Promotional Offers

ComeTransport may offer promotional pricing, introductory offers, discounts, or other Premium subscription promotions.

Promotional offers may have additional conditions, eligibility requirements, durations, or renewal terms. These conditions will be presented when applicable.

10. Failed or Declined Payments

If a recurring Premium subscription payment fails or is declined, Apple or Google may retry the payment or take other actions according to their respective billing systems.

Premium access may be restricted or discontinued if the subscription becomes inactive.

11. Account Deletion

Deleting a ComeTransport account does not necessarily cancel an active Premium subscription.

Users should cancel an active subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play before deleting their account if they do not want the subscription to renew.

Account deletion is handled separately under the ComeTransport Data Deletion Policy.

12. Premium Access After Cancellation

When a Premium subscription is cancelled, the user may continue to have access to Premium features until the end of the already-paid subscription period, depending on the applicable marketplace rules.

After the subscription period ends, Premium access may be removed.

13. Unauthorized Purchases

If a user believes that a Premium purchase was made without authorization, the user should promptly contact the applicable Apple or Google billing support service and secure the relevant account.

Users should protect their Apple ID, Google Account, device credentials, and other account information from unauthorized access.

14. Duplicate or Technical Billing Issues

If a user believes they have been charged more than once or that a subscription purchase is not correctly reflected in the ComeTransport application, the user may contact ComeTransport support for assistance with identifying the subscription status.

Where the transaction itself was processed by Apple or Google, the applicable marketplace may be responsible for transaction-level billing resolution or refunds.

15. No Direct Payment Processor

ComeTransport does not currently use Stripe, PayPal, iyzico, PayTR, or similar direct payment gateways for Premium subscriptions.

Premium mobile subscriptions are intended to be processed through Apple App Store or Google Play billing.

16. Changes to This Policy

ComeTransport may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Premium subscriptions, app marketplace requirements, applicable law, or platform functionality.

The latest version will be published through the applicable ComeTransport policy page.

17. Contact

For questions about Premium access, subscription status, or ComeTransport's application functionality, users should use the official ComeTransport support or contact method provided by the application.
