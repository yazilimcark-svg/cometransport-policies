ComeTransport

Prohibited & Restricted Items Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Purpose

This Prohibited & Restricted Items Policy explains the types of goods and materials that must not be offered, requested, arranged, or transported through ComeTransport.

ComeTransport is a platform that helps users connect regarding transportation needs and transportation services. The platform does not authorize the transportation of an item merely because a user is able to create a request or communicate with another user.

2. General Rule

Users are responsible for ensuring that all goods involved in a transportation arrangement are legal and permitted to transport under the laws applicable to the relevant locations.

If you are uncertain whether an item may legally be transported, you should not arrange its transportation through ComeTransport until you have confirmed the applicable requirements.

3. Illegal Goods

The following types of goods are prohibited where their possession, sale, distribution, or transportation is unlawful:

  • Illegal drugs and controlled substances.
  • Stolen goods or property.
  • Counterfeit goods.
  • Illegally obtained goods.
  • Goods intended to facilitate criminal activity.
  • Other goods prohibited by applicable law.

4. Weapons and Explosive Materials

Users must not use ComeTransport to arrange transportation of firearms, ammunition, explosives, or other weapons where such transportation is prohibited or restricted by applicable law.

Where transportation of a regulated item may be legally permitted, all applicable licensing, packaging, declaration, carrier, and transportation requirements must be satisfied.

5. Hazardous Materials

Dangerous or hazardous materials must not be transported through ComeTransport unless their transportation is lawful and all required safety, packaging, labeling, documentation, licensing, and carrier requirements are satisfied.

Examples may include certain:

  • Flammable materials.
  • Explosive materials.
  • Toxic substances.
  • Corrosive substances.
  • Radioactive materials.
  • Other regulated dangerous goods.

6. Human Trafficking and Exploitation

Strictly prohibited: ComeTransport must never be used to facilitate human trafficking, forced labor, exploitation, smuggling of persons, or other unlawful movement of people.

7. Wildlife and Protected Species

Users must not use ComeTransport to arrange the unlawful transportation or trade of protected wildlife, endangered species, or products derived from protected species.

8. Stolen and Counterfeit Property

Stolen property, counterfeit products, fraudulent documents, or goods intended to conceal unlawful activity are prohibited.

9. Prescription and Regulated Products

Prescription medicines, controlled medicines, medical products, and other regulated products must not be transported where such transportation is prohibited or requires authorization that has not been obtained.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable pharmaceutical, medical, customs, import, export, and transportation laws.

10. Alcohol, Tobacco, and Age-Restricted Products

Alcohol, tobacco, nicotine products, and other age-restricted goods may be subject to different laws depending on the country, state, region, or transportation route.

Users must not arrange transportation of such products where the applicable law prohibits the transaction or transportation.

11. Perishable and Temperature-Sensitive Goods

Perishable, refrigerated, frozen, or temperature-sensitive goods should only be transported when the appropriate transportation conditions can be safely and legally maintained.

Users are responsible for determining whether special packaging, temperature control, permits, labeling, or other requirements apply.

12. Cash and Financial Instruments

Users must not use ComeTransport to facilitate unlawful transportation of cash, stolen financial instruments, payment cards, or other financial items.

Transportation of legally permitted financial documents or valuables remains subject to applicable laws and the terms agreed between users.

13. Misleading Declarations

Users must not falsely describe, conceal, mislabel, or misrepresent the contents of goods in order to avoid legal, safety, customs, or transportation requirements.

Never conceal prohibited or dangerous contents. Incorrect declarations can create serious safety and legal risks.

14. Customs and Cross-Border Transportation

Where transportation crosses national borders, users are responsible for complying with applicable customs, import, export, sanctions, documentation, tax, and other border requirements.

ComeTransport does not provide customs clearance or guarantee that goods can legally cross a particular border.

15. Local Laws May Be Stricter

Laws concerning prohibited and restricted goods vary significantly between countries and regions.

An item that is lawful in one location may be restricted or prohibited in another location.

Users must comply with the laws applicable to the origin, destination, route, and transportation method involved.

16. Reporting

If you believe that a ComeTransport request or listing involves prohibited goods or unlawful activity, you should report it using available reporting tools or the official ComeTransport support channel.

17. Enforcement

ComeTransport may remove content, restrict functionality, suspend accounts, terminate accounts, or take other appropriate action when it reasonably believes that this policy or applicable law has been violated.

Where appropriate and legally permitted, ComeTransport may cooperate with relevant authorities concerning suspected unlawful activity.

18. No Guarantee of Legal Compliance

ComeTransport cannot determine the legality of every item or transportation arrangement in every country and jurisdiction.

Users remain responsible for obtaining any required professional, legal, customs, transportation, or regulatory advice.

19. Changes to This Policy

ComeTransport may update this policy when necessary to reflect changes in laws, regulations, platform functionality, or safety requirements.

20. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or need to report a potentially prohibited item, please contact ComeTransport through the official support contact method provided within the application or on the official ComeTransport website.
