Payment Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

ComeTransport has one paid service: Premium subscriptions. ComeTransport does not charge, collect, process, hold, or settle transportation fees between users.

1. Scope

This Payment Policy explains how payments for paid ComeTransport features are handled.

ComeTransport is a transportation and service marketplace that helps connect people and businesses that need transportation with people and businesses that can provide transportation services.

The platform does not operate as a payment intermediary for transportation arrangements between users.

2. Premium Subscription

ComeTransport may offer optional Premium subscriptions that provide access to additional digital features or functionality within the application.

Premium is optional. Users can use the portions of ComeTransport made available without a Premium subscription according to the applicable product design and terms.

3. App Store and Google Play Billing

Premium subscriptions purchased through the ComeTransport mobile application are processed through the applicable mobile application marketplace.

  • Apple devices: Apple App Store and Apple's in-app purchase system.
  • Android devices: Google Play and Google Play's billing system.

ComeTransport does not directly process credit card or debit card transactions for Premium subscriptions inside its own payment system.

4. No Direct Payment Processing by ComeTransport

ComeTransport does not provide its own payment gateway for Premium subscriptions.

ComeTransport does not directly request or store users' full credit card or debit card numbers for Premium subscription purchases.

Payment credentials and transaction processing for mobile subscriptions are handled by the applicable Apple or Google payment infrastructure according to their respective terms and policies.

5. No Transportation Fees

ComeTransport does not charge users a transportation fee. ComeTransport does not collect transportation payments from users.

There is no transportation payment system within ComeTransport.

ComeTransport does not:

  • Collect transportation fees.
  • Hold transportation funds.
  • Transfer transportation payments between users.
  • Take a commission from transportation fees.
  • Provide escrow for transportation arrangements.
  • Process card payments between transportation requesters and providers.
  • Settle transportation invoices between users.

Any private commercial or other arrangement between users is outside ComeTransport's payment system and is not processed through the ComeTransport application.

6. No Commission on Transportation

ComeTransport does not charge a percentage-based commission on transportation services arranged between users.

ComeTransport's Premium subscription is separate from any transportation arrangement between users.

7. Subscription Price

The applicable Premium subscription price will be displayed to the user before the subscription is purchased.

Prices may vary depending on the user's country, currency, applicable taxes, subscription plan, promotional offers, and the pricing rules of the applicable app marketplace.

8. Automatic Renewal

Premium subscriptions may be offered as automatically renewing subscriptions where supported by the applicable App Store or Google Play billing system.

If a subscription automatically renews, the applicable marketplace may charge the user's selected payment method according to the subscription terms displayed at the time of purchase.

Users are responsible for reviewing the renewal terms before subscribing.

9. Cancelling a Premium Subscription

Users can manage or cancel their Premium subscription through the applicable Apple or Google subscription management system.

Deleting the ComeTransport account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription.

Users should cancel an active subscription through the applicable marketplace if they do not want it to renew.

10. Refunds

Because Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play, refund requests may be subject to the applicable marketplace rules and procedures.

ComeTransport does not independently guarantee refunds for purchases processed by Apple or Google.

11. Taxes

Applicable taxes, including sales tax, VAT, GST, or other similar taxes, may be included in or added to the displayed subscription price depending on the user's location and the applicable marketplace rules.

12. Promotional Offers

ComeTransport may offer promotional pricing, introductory periods, free trials, or other Premium subscription offers where permitted.

Any special conditions, duration, renewal terms, or eligibility requirements will be presented to users where applicable.

13. Free Trials

If ComeTransport offers a free trial, the applicable trial duration and renewal conditions will be displayed before activation.

A free trial may automatically convert into a paid subscription if the user does not cancel according to the applicable marketplace's rules.

14. Payment Information

ComeTransport does not need to receive a user's complete payment card details to provide Premium subscription functionality through Apple or Google.

Users should review Apple's and Google's respective privacy and payment practices for information about how payment information is handled by those services.

15. Subscription Access

Premium features may become available after a successful subscription transaction has been confirmed by the applicable app marketplace and the ComeTransport application has recognized the user's subscription status.

Access to Premium features may end when a subscription expires, is cancelled, refunded, revoked, or otherwise becomes inactive.

16. Changes to Premium Pricing

ComeTransport may change Premium subscription pricing or introduce new subscription plans in accordance with applicable law and the rules of the relevant app marketplace.

Any applicable change to an existing subscription will be handled in accordance with the applicable Apple or Google subscription rules.

17. Third-Party Billing Platforms

ComeTransport does not currently use third-party payment processors such as Stripe, PayPal, iyzico, PayTR, or similar direct payment gateways for Premium subscriptions.

Premium mobile subscriptions are intended to be handled through Apple App Store or Google Play billing.

18. Payment Disputes

Questions concerning a transaction processed directly by Apple or Google may need to be addressed through the applicable marketplace's support and billing procedures.

ComeTransport may assist users with general questions concerning Premium access or subscription recognition within the application.

19. Security

ComeTransport takes reasonable measures to protect information under its control. However, payment processing for Premium subscriptions is performed through third-party marketplace infrastructure.

Users should protect their Apple ID, Google Account, device credentials, and other account information from unauthorized access.

20. Relationship With Other Policies

This Payment Policy should be read together with the ComeTransport Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and other applicable policies.

If there is a conflict between this Payment Policy and a specific subscription disclosure shown at the time of purchase, the specific purchase disclosure may apply to that transaction to the extent permitted by applicable law.

21. Changes to This Payment Policy

ComeTransport may update this Payment Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Premium subscriptions, app marketplace requirements, applicable law, or the functionality of the platform.

The updated version will be published on the applicable ComeTransport policy page.

22. Contact

For questions regarding Premium subscriptions or payment-related functionality within ComeTransport, users should use the official ComeTransport support or contact method provided by the application.
